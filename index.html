The company

Pigrosoft is a one-person software company in Berlin. It is run by Jeff Humble, a product designer who spent fifteen years making software for other people and now makes small tools for himself and anyone with the same problems.

Every tool follows the same house rules.

  • One tool, one job.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Runs in your browser, and offline where it can.
  • Pay once and keep it. No subscriptions.

Help

Something is broken. What do I do?

Email support@workshoptimers.com and say which tool you were using, what you were doing, and which browser you were in. A screenshot helps a lot.

I paid for something and lost my license key.

Search your inbox for the receipt from Polar, our payment provider, because the key is in that email. If it is gone, write to us from the address you paid with and we will sort it out.

Can I get a refund?

Each tool lists its own terms. For Workshop Timers, see the refund page.

Do you track me?

No. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs IP addresses the way any web host does. The privacy page has the details.

Why a pig?

Pigro is Italian for lazy, and good software lets you be lazy. The pig is a bonus. Click it and see what happens.
